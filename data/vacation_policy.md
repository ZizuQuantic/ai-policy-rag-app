# Corporate Policy: Paid Time Off (PTO) and Vacation

## 1. Overview
This policy outlines the guidelines and procedures for requesting and taking Paid Time Off (PTO) at our organization. The company believes that personal time off is essential for maintaining productivity, health, and work-life balance.

## 2. Annual Accrual Rate
All full-time employees accrue PTO monthly, starting from their first day of employment. 
* Employees with 0-2 years of service accrue 15 days of PTO per calendar year.
* Employees with 3-5 years of service accrue 20 days of PTO per calendar year.
* Employees with more than 5 years of service accrue 25 days of PTO per calendar year.

## 3. Request and Approval Process
Employees must submit all PTO requests through the HR portal at least two (2) weeks in advance for planned vacations lasting more than three consecutive days. For short-term absences (1-2 days), a 48-hour notice is required. 
* Supervisors must approve or deny the request within 3 business days of submission.
* In case of emergencies or sudden illness, employees must notify their manager before 9:00 AM on the day of absence.

## 4. Carry-Over Rules
A maximum of five (5) unused PTO days may be carried over into the next calendar year. Any additional unused PTO beyond these 5 days will be forfeited on December 31st. No financial compensation will be provided for forfeited days.
