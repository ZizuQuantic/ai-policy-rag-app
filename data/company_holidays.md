# Corporate Policy: Paid Company Holidays

## 1. Overview
The company observes specific paid holidays each calendar year. All full-time and part-time regular employees are eligible for holiday pay from their date of hire.

## 2. Observed Holidays
The following ten (10) days are official paid company holidays:
1. New Year's Day (January 1)
2. Memorial Day (Last Monday in May)
3. Independence Day (July 4)
4. Labor Day (First Monday in September)
5. Thanksgiving Day (Fourth Thursday in November)
6. Day after Thanksgiving (Fourth Friday in November)
7. Christmas Eve (December 24)
8. Christmas Day (December 25)
9. New Year's Eve (December 31)
10. Floating Holiday (1 day of employee's choice, requires 30 days prior notice)

## 3. Holiday Pay Eligibility
To receive holiday pay, employees must work their regularly scheduled shift immediately before and immediately after the holiday, unless they have an approved PTO request in place.
