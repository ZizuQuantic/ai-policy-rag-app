# Corporate Policy: Attendance and Leave Policy
## Purpose
Regular attendance and punctuality are essential to maintaining productivity and ensuring efficient operations.
## Attendance Expectations
Employees are expected to:
- Report to work as scheduled
- Arrive on time
- Remain available during working hours
- Follow attendance reporting procedures
## Absence Reporting
Employees who cannot attend work must:
- Notify their supervisor as soon as possible
- Provide an estimated return date
- Submit supporting documentation when required
## Types of Leave
### Annual Leave
Annual leave allows employees to rest and maintain work-life balance.
Leave requests should:
- Be submitted in advance
- Receive supervisor approval
- Consider operational requirements
### Sick Leave
Employees who are unable to work due to illness or injury may use sick leave.
Medical documentation may be required for extended absences.
### Parental Leave
Eligible employees may receive maternity, paternity, or adoption leave in accordance with applicable laws and Company policies.
### Bereavement Leave
Employees may be granted leave following the death of an immediate family member.
### Unpaid Leave
Employees may request unpaid leave under special circumstances subject to management approval.
### Attendance Monitoring
The Company may track attendance records to ensure fairness and operational effectiveness.
### Excessive Absenteeism
Repeated lateness or unexcused absences may result in:
- Coaching
- Corrective action
- Formal disciplinary measures
