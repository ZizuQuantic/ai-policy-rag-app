# Corporate Policy: Expense Reimbursement

## 1. Overview
This policy establishes the rules for business-related expenses incurred by employees on behalf of the company. The company will reimburse reasonable and necessary expenses that are directly related to business operations.

## 2. Travel Expenses
* **Air Travel:** All flights must be booked in Economy Class. Business or First Class tickets will not be reimbursed unless pre-approved in writing by the CFO.
* **Lodging:** Standard hotel rooms up to $250 per night are automatically approved. Higher rates require managerial sign-off before booking.
* **Meals:** The maximum daily allowance (per diem) for meals during business travel is $75. Receipts are required for any single meal exceeding $25.

## 3. Submission Process
Employees must submit expense reports via the Expensify portal within thirty (30) days of incurring the expense. Itemized receipts must be attached for all expenses. Late submissions beyond 60 days will be automatically rejected, and the employee will not be reimbursed.
