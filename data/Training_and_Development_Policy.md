# Corporate Policy: Training and Development Policy
## Purpose
The Company recognizes that employee development contributes to individual success and organizational growth. Continuous learning helps employees improve performance, increase job satisfaction, and prepare for future responsibilities.
## Scope
This policy applies to all employees who are eligible for training opportunities.
## Types of Training
### Mandatory Training
Employees may be required to complete training in areas such as:
- Workplace safety
- Information security
- Data privacy
- Anti-harassment
- Compliance requirements
Completion of mandatory training is a condition of employment.
### Professional Development
Employees may participate in:
- Industry conferences
- Workshops
- Online courses
- Certification programs
- Leadership development programs
### Cross-Functional Training
The Company may provide opportunities for employees to learn skills outside their primary roles to enhance flexibility and collaboration.
### Development Planning
Managers and employees should work together to create development plans that include:
- Career goals
- Skill gaps
- Training objectives
- Progress reviews
### Training Reimbursement
The Company may reimburse approved educational expenses, including:
- Course fees
- Examination fees
- Training materials
Conditions may include:
- Prior approval
- Successful completion
- Continued employment for a specified period
## Employee Responsibilities
Employees are expected to:
- Attend scheduled training
- Actively participate
- Apply learned skills in their work
- Share knowledge when appropriate
### Evaluation
Training effectiveness may be assessed through:
- Feedback surveys
- Performance improvements
- Certification results
- Manager evaluations
