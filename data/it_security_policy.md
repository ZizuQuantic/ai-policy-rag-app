# Corporate Policy: Information Technology and Cybersecurity

## 1. Overview
This policy outlines the security rules for using company-owned IT assets, networks, and data. Protecting corporate data from cyber threats is the responsibility of every employee.

## 2. Password Standards
All employee accounts must use strong passwords that meet the following criteria:
* Minimum of 12 characters.
* Must include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters (e.g., !, @, #).
* Passwords must be changed every ninety (90) days.
* Reusing any of the previous four (4) passwords is strictly prohibited.

## 3. Device Management
Employees must never leave their laptops unattended in public places. When leaving their desks at the office, employees must lock their screens (Windows Key + L or Command + Control + Q). Software installations not approved by the IT department are strictly forbidden.
