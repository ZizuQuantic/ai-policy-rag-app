# Corporate Policy: Compensation and Benefits Policy
## Purpose
The Company seeks to attract, retain, and motivate talented employees by providing fair, competitive, and equitable compensation and benefits.
## Compensation Philosophy
The Company determines compensation based on:
- Job responsibilities
- Qualifications
- Experience
- Performance
- Market conditions
- Internal pay equity
## Salary Administration
### Salary Structure
Employees are assigned salary ranges based on:
- Position level
- Responsibilities
- Market benchmarks
### Salary Reviews
Compensation reviews may occur annually and may consider:
- Individual performance
- Department performance
- Company financial results
- Market salary trends
Salary increases are not guaranteed.
### Incentive Programs
Eligible employees may participate in:
- Performance bonuses
- Sales commissions
- Profit-sharing plans
- Recognition awards
## Benefits
Subject to eligibility requirements, benefits may include:
### Health Benefits
- Medical insurance
- Dental insurance
- Vision insurance
### Financial Benefits
- Retirement plans
- Pension contributions
- Life insurance
### Leave Benefits
- Annual leave
- Sick leave
- Parental leave
- Bereavement leave
### Employee Assistance Programs
The Company may provide confidential support services for personal or work-related challenges.
## Confidentiality and Compliance
Compensation information will be managed securely and disclosed only to authorized individuals.
