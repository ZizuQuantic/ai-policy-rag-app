# Corporate Policy: Privacy and Data Protection Policy
## Purpose
The Company is committed to protecting personal information, confidential business data, and customer information from unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss.
## Scope
This policy applies to:
- Employee information
- Customer information
- Vendor information
- Financial records
- Business documents
- Electronic and physical records
## Data Protection Principles
### Lawful Collection
Information will be collected only for legitimate business purposes.
### Data Minimization
Only information necessary for business operations will be collected and retained.
### Accuracy
Employees responsible for maintaining records must ensure information remains accurate and current.
### Security
Appropriate measures will be implemented to protect information from:
- Unauthorized access
- Data breaches
- Loss
- Theft
- Alteration
## Employee Responsibilities
Employees must:
- Protect confidential information
- Follow password requirements
- Use approved systems and applications
- Secure physical and electronic records
- Report suspected security incidents immediately
### Access Control
Access to confidential information will be limited to authorized personnel with legitimate business needs.
Employees must not access information unrelated to their responsibilities.
### Data Retention
Information will be retained only for as long as necessary to:
- Meet business requirements
- Comply with legal obligations
- Resolve disputes
- Enforce agreements
### Data Breach Response
Employees must immediately report any suspected breach involving:
- Lost devices
- Unauthorized access
- Misdirected emails
- Suspicious activity
The Company will investigate incidents and take corrective action.
### Consequences of Violations
Failure to comply with this policy may result in:
- Disciplinary action
- Termination of employment
- Legal liability
- Regulatory penalties
