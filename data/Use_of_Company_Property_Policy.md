# Corporate Policy: Use of Company Property Policy
## Purpose
Company property is provided to employees to support business operations. This policy establishes rules for proper use, protection, and management of Company assets.
## Scope
Company property includes:
- Computers and laptops
- Mobile phones
- Software licenses
- Office furniture
- Company vehicles
- Company email accounts
- Internet access
- Security access cards
- Tools and equipment
## Employee Responsibilities
Employees are expected to:
- Use Company property responsibly
- Protect assets from theft, damage, and misuse
- Follow security procedures
- Report malfunctioning equipment promptly
## Proper Use
Company property should primarily be used for business activities.
Limited personal use may be permitted provided it:
- Does not interfere with work duties
- Does not create additional costs
- Does not violate Company policies
## Information Technology Resources
Employees must not:
- Share passwords
- Install unauthorized software
- Download illegal content
- Access inappropriate websites
- Disable security controls
## Protection of Assets
Employees are responsible for safeguarding equipment assigned to them.
This includes:
- Locking devices when unattended
- Keeping equipment secure during travel
- Following data security procedures
## Monitoring and Privacy
The Company reserves the right to monitor Company-owned systems, networks, and devices to ensure compliance with policies and legal obligations.
Employees should have no expectation of personal privacy when using Company-owned resources except where prohibited by law.
## Return of Property
Upon resignation, termination, or transfer, employees must return all Company property in good condition.
Failure to return property may result in deductions permitted by law or legal action.
