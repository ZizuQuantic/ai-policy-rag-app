# Corporate Policy: Remote Work and Telecommuting

## 1. Overview
This policy defines the guidelines and expectations for employees who work remotely, either full-time or on a hybrid schedule. The goal is to provide flexibility while maintaining high productivity and communication standards.

## 2. Eligibility
Remote work eligibility is determined by the department head and HR based on the nature of the employee's role. Positions that require physical presence (e.g., office reception, hardware maintenance) are not eligible for remote work. Employees must maintain a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or higher to qualify.

## 3. Work Hours and Availability
Remote employees are expected to core work hours between 10:00 AM and 4:00 PM EST, regardless of their local time zone. During these hours, employees must be reachable via Slack, email, and Microsoft Teams. Response times to internal messages should not exceed two (2) hours during work schedules.

## 4. Equipment and Security
The company provides a standard laptop, monitor, and keyboard. Employees are responsible for providing a secure, high-speed internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps download speed). Employees must never use public Wi-Fi without connecting to the company's Virtual Private Network (VPN).
